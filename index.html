HaliCare Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 26, 2025

1. Introduction

HaliCare is a mobile application and web platform designed to connect HIV/AIDS patients in Kenya with operational healthcare clinics and counseling centers. Our mission is to restore continuity of care by providing a reliable channel for locating services, booking appointments, and receiving essential support.

We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal and health information. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and your rights under the Kenyan Data Protection Act, 2019, the Health Act, 2017, and the Digital Health Act, 2023.

By using HaliCare, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

To provide our services, we collect different types of information from and about our users, who include patients and clinicians.

  • Personal Information: When you register, we collect information such as your full name and phone number. This is used to create and manage your account and for communication purposes.
  • Sensitive Health Information: With your explicit consent, we collect information necessary for your care. This includes your HIV status, medication needs, appointment history, and counseling requirements. This is classified as "sensitive personal data" under the Kenyan Data Protection Act and is handled with the highest level of security.
  • Location Data: We collect precise geolocation data from your mobile device to provide our core feature of locating nearby operational clinics and counseling centers. This data is used only for this purpose and is not stored longer than necessary.
  • Usage and Analytics Data: We collect information on how you interact with our app and dashboard. For clinicians, this includes data on appointment trends and service usage. This data is aggregated and anonymized to help us improve our services, ensure system performance, and provide insights to clinics.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and Manage Our Core Service: To connect you with clinics, manage your bookings, and display real-time clinic status and services.
  • Facilitate Appointments: To schedule, modify, and cancel appointments, and to send notifications and reminders to both patients and clinics.
  • Ensure Security and Compliance: To enforce role-based access control, log activities for security audits, and comply with our legal obligations under Kenyan health and data protection laws.
  • Improve Our Platform: To analyze anonymized usage data to enhance the user experience, fix bugs, and improve the performance and reliability of the HaliCare platform.

4. How We Share Your Information

Your privacy is a priority. We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We only share your information in the following circumstances:

  • With Clinics: We share your relevant personal and health information with the specific clinic or counseling center you book an appointment with. This is necessary to provide you with the healthcare services you have requested. The clinic will only have access to the information needed for your scheduled appointment.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose your information if required to do so by law, court order, or other government authority, or to protect our rights, property, or safety, or that of our users or the public.
  • Service Providers: We may share data with trusted third-party service providers (e.g., cloud hosting providers) who perform services on our behalf. These providers are contractually obligated to protect your data and are not permitted to use it for any other purpose.

5. Data Security

We implement robust technical and organizational security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include:

  • Encryption: All data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted using HTTPS. Sensitive data stored on our servers is also encrypted.
  • Access Control: We use strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) to ensure that only authorized personnel (e.g., your assigned clinician) can access your health information.
  • Audit Logs: We maintain logs of system access and activities to monitor for and prevent unauthorized access.
  • Regular Audits: We conduct periodic security audits and penetration testing to identify and address vulnerabilities in our system.

6. Your Rights

Under the Data Protection Act, you have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectification: Request correction of any inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Erasure: Request the deletion of your personal data, subject to certain legal obligations.
  • Object: Object to the processing of your data in certain circumstances.
  • Withdraw Consent: Where we rely on your consent for processing, you have the right to withdraw it at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at the details provided below.

7. Data Retention

We will only retain your personal information for as long as is necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, including to comply with legal, accounting, or reporting requirements.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Effective Date" above. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
